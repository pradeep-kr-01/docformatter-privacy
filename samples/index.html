Sample files for trying DocFormatter AI

Two small Word files: a fictional manuscript and a journal template. Download both on your phone, then choose them in the app.

sample-manuscript.docx The manuscript: a short fictional paper, typed plainly in Calibri sample-template.docx The template: a journal style in Times New Roman, with capitalised headings
  1. Sign in to the app.
  2. Choose sample-manuscript.docx as your document and sample-template.docx as the template.
  3. Format, then download the result. It is set in the template's style.
