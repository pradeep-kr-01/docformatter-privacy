Privacy Policy — DocFormatter AI

Last updated: 24 September 2026 Contact: pradeepkr848115@gmail.com

This policy describes what DocFormatter AI collects, why, and what happens to it. It describes what the software actually does, not what it might do — the behaviour below can be checked against the source.

The short version

  • Your manuscript is processed and deleted. It is never stored, never read by a human, and never sent to any third-party AI service.
  • We hold an email address, a hashed password, and a record of credits and payments.
  • We do not run analytics, advertising, or any tracking across apps or sites.
  • Card details never reach our servers.

What we collect

Your account

Data Why Kept
Email address To identify your account, send password resets and receipts Until you delete your account
Password To sign you in. Stored only as a bcrypt hash — we cannot read it or recover it Until you delete your account

Your documents

When you format a document, the manuscript and the template are uploaded, processed, and deleted as soon as the request finishes. The formatted result is deleted about a minute after it is sent to you.

We do not keep copies, we do not index the contents, and no person reads them. Nothing in your document is sent to any third-party service, including any AI or language model. The formatting engine is a rule-based program running on our own server.

Credits and payments

Data Why Kept
Credit ledger — grants, purchases, use To show your balance and explain it if you query it Life of the account
Payment records — Razorpay order and payment ids, amount, plan To credit your purchase and meet tax and accounting obligations As long as the law requires, typically 7 years, even after account deletion

Card and UPI details never reach us. Payment is handled entirely by Razorpay on their own page. We receive only a confirmation that a payment succeeded, and an identifier for it.

Technical data

Our servers keep standard access logs, which include IP addresses, for security and to stop abuse — for example, limiting how many times a password can be guessed. These are not linked to your documents.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers. No location. No contacts, photos, calendar or files beyond the documents you deliberately upload. No analytics SDK. No tracking of you across other apps or websites. The app requests only one Android permission: internet access.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising. Nobody receives your documents.

Your choices

  • See your data — your balance and full credit history are in the app, under your account.
  • Correct it — change your password in the app; email us to change your address.
  • Delete your account — from the account screen in the app, or by emailing pradeepkr848115@gmail.com. Deleting removes your email, password hash, credit balance and history. Payment records are retained where the law requires, reduced to the minimum needed for accounting.
  • Unused credits are not refunded on deletion, so spend them first.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, never stored in a readable form. Sessions use short-lived tokens; the long-lived one is rotated on every use, and presenting an old one signs the account out everywhere, on the assumption it was stolen. Traffic is HTTPS throughout.

On your phone, the long-lived token is kept in encrypted storage, with the key held in the Android Keystore and bound to the device. Copying the file off the phone yields ciphertext that cannot be decrypted anywhere else. Your password is never stored on the device at all. The email address the sign-in screen remembers is kept in ordinary app storage, since it is not a secret.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account has been accessed by someone else, change your password — that signs out every other device — and tell us.

Children

This service is for researchers and students preparing academic work. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly hold their data.

Changes

Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
